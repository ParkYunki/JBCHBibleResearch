# Language card: en — English
- Target Bible: BSB (Berean Standard Bible)
- "Textus Receptus" term: Textus Receptus
- "critical text" term: critical text
- "close" sentence: Greek → `Close to the Greek.` / Hebrew → `Close to the Hebrew.`
- Style: Use “ ” quotes. LITERAL field is always `=`.

Examples (Philemon 2, 12, 18, 25 — pilot output, TSV):
```
2	=	BSB adds “that meets” to “the church at your house” (κατ᾽ οἶκόν σου) and supplies “our” before “sister”; the Greek says “the sister.” LSV’s “the beloved” follows the Textus Receptus (ἀγαπητῇ).
12	=	BSB renders τὰ ἐμὰ σπλάγχνα (“my own inner parts”) as “my very heart.” Like the critical text, it has no “receive him” (προσλαβοῦ), which appears in the Textus Receptus.
18	=	Close to the Greek; “charge this to me” (ἐμοὶ ἐλλόγα) becomes “charge it to my account.”
25	=	BSB follows the critical text: “the Lord” without “our,” and no “Amen.” “Our,” “Amen,” and the closing note (“written to Philemon from Rome by Onesimus”) come from the Textus Receptus.
```
