## 19:109:5

3 רָעָה = evil (distress)
4 תַּחַת = in place of
5 טוֹבָה = good (welfare)
6 וְשִׂנְאָה = and hatred (hating)
7 תַּחַת = in place of
8 אַהֲבָתִי = love my (love)

## 19:109:6

## 19:109:9
