## 18:6:12

2 כֹּחַ = [the] strength of
3 אֲבָנִים = stones (stone)

5 אִם־ = or?
6 בְּשָׂרִי = [is] flesh my (flesh)
7 נָחוּשׁ = bronze

## 18:6:14
